Web Developer Job Criteria
Proficient in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including frameworks such as React, Angular, or Vue.js.

Experience with server-side languages such as Python, PHP, Ruby, or Node.js.

Understanding of RESTful APIs and integration with front-end applications.

Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git.

Ability to design responsive and user-friendly web interfaces.

Experience with databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, or MongoDB.

Knowledge of security best practices for web applications.

Strong problem-solving and debugging skills.

Good communication and teamwork abilities.

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field (preferred).

These criteria are widely used across the industry to evaluate candidates for web developer positions.

================================================================================
ETHICAL HIRING PLATFORM — RESEARCH DOCUMENT
End-to-End Features, Workflow, and Extended Identity Verification
================================================================================
Generated for academic / technical paper use. Describes the implemented system
as reflected in the Django codebase, and documents the Secure Candidate Identity
Verification and Anti-Spoofing Module: a mandatory pre-screening stage before
video interview submission (Aadhaar reference face, liveness, and face matching).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Ethical Hiring Platform is a web application that automates parts of early-
stage hiring while keeping human oversight in the loop. Candidates authenticate,
submit a résumé (PDF), and the system extracts structured profile data and scores
the candidate against employer-uploaded criteria using retrieval-augmented LLM
pipelines. Administrators review scores and approve or reject candidates.
Approved candidates must pass the Secure Candidate Identity Verification and
Anti-Spoofing Module before they can access the video interview recording
interface; then they complete an AI-generated video Q&A whose responses are
transcribed and scored against model answers. The identity module ties live
presence to an Aadhaar-derived reference face, reduces impersonation and replay
risk, and records audit metadata while applying privacy-aware handling of images.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM GOALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Reduce manual résumé parsing and first-pass filtering time.
- Apply consistent, criteria-based scoring using the employer’s job requirements.
- Generate role-relevant interview questions from the candidate’s own résumé.
- Assess video responses with speech-to-text and LLM-based comparison scoring.
- Support asynchronous operations (heavy AI and media work) via background jobs.
- Enforce a mandatory biometric gate: Aadhaar image processing, reference face
  embedding, liveness/anti-spoofing, and face similarity verification before
  unlocking video interview submission.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Web framework: Django (Python), with session-based authentication and optional
  Google/social login (django-allauth / social-auth-app-django).
- Task queue: Celery with Redis as broker and result backend.
- AI / NLP:
  - LlamaIndex for document loading, vector + keyword retrieval, and querying.
  - Google Gemini (e.g. gemini-2.5-flash) for structured extraction, scoring,
    question generation, and video-answer evaluation.
  - Local Hugging Face embeddings (e.g. BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) for retrieval.
- Media: FFmpeg for audio extraction from recorded video responses.
- Speech-to-text pipeline (video answers): prefers SpeechRecognition (free path
  on supported Python versions), with fallbacks to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text
  and AssemblyAI when configured.
- Email: SMTP (e.g. Gmail) for shortlist notifications.
- Deployment: Docker-friendly (Python 3.12 base image, FFmpeg in container).
- Identity verification (pre–video interview): Django APIs and session gating;
  DeepFace for detection, embeddings, verification, and anti-spoof/liveness;
  EasyOCR for text-region detection on Aadhaar with masking; OpenCV, Pillow,
  NumPy for image pipelines; tf-keras as DeepFace backend support; browser
  capture via MediaDevices/getUserMedia, Canvas, and Fetch (see Section 7).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATA MODELS (CORE ENTITIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 hiring_app — Candidate profile and résumé pipeline
    - ResumeDetails: Per-user structured fields (name, institution, city, pass-
      out year, CGPA, degree, skills, experience, projects, achievements, contact),
      multi-dimensional scores (tech skills, experience/achievements,
      certifications, projects, aggregate score, video_score), and approval
      status (pending / approved / rejected).
    - ResumeCheck: Tracks whether the user has completed PDF upload.
    - FileResumePath: Stores filesystem path to the user’s uploaded résumé folder.

4.2 screening — Video interview artifacts
    - Question: Text of an interview question, per user, with status attended /
      not_attended.
    - VideoRecording: Uploaded video per question; optional extracted audio;
      transcript_text after speech-to-text.

4.3 screening / identity — Face reference and verification artifacts
    - FaceReference (Django model): persists verification state per candidate,
      including Aadhaar image (ImageField), derived face crop, webcam face crop,
      face embedding (binary bytes), verification timestamp, and related metadata
      for audit. Server-side processing crops the facial region from the Aadhaar
      image, generates a persistent embedding, and masks non-face textual
      regions (EasyOCR-assisted) to limit exposure of personally identifiable
      text on the document.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. END-TO-END WORKFLOW (AS IMPLEMENTED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Candidate journey
    A. Registration / login
       - User signs in via username/password and/or Google OAuth (configured in
         settings).

    B. Résumé upload (home)
       - Non-admin users upload a single PDF résumé to a user-specific directory.
       - ResumeCheck.upload_status is set to indicate upload completion.
       - After upload, flow redirects toward success/processing.

    C. Structured extraction (success view)
       - If ResumeDetails does not yet exist for the user, the PDF folder is read
         via LlamaIndex; vector and keyword indices are built; a constrained
         prompt asks the LLM to return exactly twelve comma-separated fields
         (name through phone).
       - Parsed values populate a new ResumeDetails row with status "pending".

    D. Awaiting employer scoring and decision
       - Candidate sees a status page (response) while status is not "approved".
       - When status becomes "approved", the candidate enters the identity
         verification flow before the video screening UI is unlocked.

    D2. Secure Candidate Identity Verification and Anti-Spoofing (mandatory)
       - Step 1 — Aadhaar upload: POST to /screen/upload_aadhaar/. Server detects
         and crops the face, generates a persistent embedding, masks textual
         regions on the ID image for privacy, and stores reference artifacts via
         FaceReference / media storage.
       - Step 2 — Live webcam verification: browser captures a real-time frame
         (getUserMedia, Canvas), sends it to /screen/verify_face/. Backend runs
         (1) liveness/anti-spoof detection (replay, photo/screen attacks), then
         (2) face similarity match against the Aadhaar-derived reference. A
         verification score is computed; only scores above the configured
         threshold unlock access to the interview recording interface. Raw webcam
         frames are removed after processing where policy dictates; verification
         metadata (e.g. timestamp, face artifacts) is retained for auditability.

    E. Video screening (screening app)
       - Questions were created when an admin approved the candidate (see admin
         flow). The candidate sees the first unanswered question.
       - For each question, the client uploads a video; server stores VideoRecording,
         marks Question as attended, and queues audio extraction.
       - Celery extracts audio (FFmpeg), runs transcription, and stores transcript.

    F. Completion
       - When all questions are answered, completed_task triggers generate_score:
         for each answer, an ideal answer is synthesized, then the transcript is
         compared and a score out of 100 is parsed; an average becomes
         ResumeDetails.video_score.

5.2 Administrator / recruiter journey
    A. Admin dashboard
       - Superuser views all ResumeDetails entries linked to users.

    B. Job criteria upload (upload_creteria)
       - Admin uploads a criteria document; copies are placed in each candidate’s
         résumé directory path; for each user, get_score Celery task is queued.

    C. Automated résumé scoring (get_score task)
       - LlamaIndex query engine reads the candidate folder (résumé + criteria).
       - Gemini returns a dictionary with percentages (0–100) for: Tech Skills,
         Experience & Achievements, Certifications, Projects.
       - Scores and a derived aggregate are written to the latest ResumeDetails
         for that user.

    D. Communication
       - Admin can queue send_mail_user to email the candidate regarding
         shortlisting (next-round notification).

    E. Approve / reject
       - approve: sets ResumeDetails status to "approved" and queues
         generating_questions with the user’s résumé path—Gemini produces ~3
         skill-focused questions stored as Question rows (not_attended).
       - reject: sets status to "rejected".

7. SECURE CANDIDATE IDENTITY VERIFICATION AND ANTI-SPOOFING MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Module overview (narrative)

The platform introduces a Secure Candidate Identity Verification and Anti-
Spoofing Module as a mandatory pre-screening stage before video interview
submission. In this feature, the candidate first uploads an Aadhaar card image,
which is processed server-side to detect and crop the facial region, generate a
persistent face embedding, and mask non-face textual regions to reduce exposure
of personally identifiable information. After reference creation, the candidate
performs live webcam verification: the browser captures a real-time frame, sends
it to the backend, and the system executes two checks in sequence—(1) liveness/
anti-spoof detection to identify replay attacks (e.g., photo/screen), and (2)
face similarity matching against the Aadhaar-derived reference face. A
verification score is produced, and only candidates crossing the configured
threshold are allowed to unlock and access the interview recording interface.
The module also records verification metadata (timestamp and face artifacts) for
auditability while applying privacy cleanup (removal of raw webcam image after
processing). This design improves authenticity assurance in remote hiring,
reduces impersonation risk, and provides a privacy-aware biometric gate aligned
with real-world recruitment constraints.

7.2 Placement in the end-to-end workflow

    1) Résumé upload → parsing → pending admin review.
    2) Admin scoring + approve (and question generation).
    3) Mandatory identity module: /screen/upload_aadhaar/ then /screen/verify_face/.
    4) On PASS (score ≥ threshold): unlock /screen/ interview flow (screen,
       upload_video, completed).
    5) On FAIL or inconclusive: block recording UI; optional retry limits or
       manual review per policy.

7.3 Tech stack used in this feature

    Backend framework: Django (request handling, API endpoints, session gating,
    model persistence).

    Computer vision / face AI: DeepFace (face detection, embedding generation,
    verification, anti-spoofing/liveness).

    OCR + privacy masking: EasyOCR (text region detection on Aadhaar for masking).

    Image processing: OpenCV (cv2) + Pillow (PIL) + NumPy (image conversion,
    drawing masks, face array handling).

    ML runtime dependency: tf-keras (DeepFace backend support).

    Database layer: Django ORM with FaceReference model (stores Aadhaar image,
    face crop, webcam face crop, embedding bytes, verification time).

    Frontend: HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript (2-step UX, webcam capture, async
    verification flow).

    Browser APIs: MediaDevices/getUserMedia, Canvas API, Fetch API (live camera
    access, frame capture, JSON upload).

    Storage: Django ImageField media storage (reference and verification
    artifacts).

    Routing / endpoints added: /screen/upload_aadhaar/ and /screen/verify_face/.

7.4 Additional functional and non-functional requirements

    - Fraud and quality: document glare/blur hints, face occlusion, multiple-face
      detection; configurable thresholds for auto-pass vs. manual review.
    - Privacy and compliance: encrypt sensitive media at rest where required;
      minimize retention of raw frames; obtain explicit consent for biometric and
      Aadhaar processing; align with applicable law (e.g. India DPDP, UIDAI /
      Aadhaar usage norms where relevant).
    - Operations: rate limiting on verification endpoints; monitoring and
      appeal path for false rejects; fairness review of threshold settings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. ETHICAL AND GOVERNANCE NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Résumé scoring and video scoring use LLMs; outcomes should be disclosed to
  candidates; human review remains important for edge cases and fairness.
- Aadhaar and biometric processing are high-risk: lawful basis, explicit consent,
  transparent policy, secure storage, data minimization (masking, deletion of raw
  webcam images post-processing), and compliance with UIDAI / local regulations.
- Face recognition errors can disproportionately affect some demographics;
  human appeal paths and threshold tuning with fairness evaluation are essential.
- The “ethical” positioning of the platform should explicitly cover AI decision
  support (not sole decision-making), data retention, and candidate consent for
  Aadhaar and biometric processing used in the identity verification module.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Ethical Hiring Platform implements a closed loop from authenticated résumé
submission through criteria-based AI scoring, human shortlisting, and
personalized question generation. The Secure Candidate Identity Verification and
Anti-Spoofing Module—Aadhaar reference embedding, EasyOCR-assisted masking,
DeepFace liveness and face match, and threshold-gated session access—provides a
privacy-aware mandatory stage before asynchronous video responses and
transcript-based AI scoring. Together, this closes a critical trust gap in remote
hiring by tying the interview session to a verified live presence while limiting
unnecessary exposure of ID text and transient camera frames. Deployment requires
strict privacy controls, fairness review, regulatory alignment for Aadhaar and
biometrics, and clear candidate-facing policies.
